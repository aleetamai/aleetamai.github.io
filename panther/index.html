Panther

A workspace for organizing the entire life of your research projects.

Currently in development

The project

Research is made of projects, not isolated files.

A research project is much more than a paper. It evolves through drafts, presentations, coauthors, submissions, conferences, revisions, deadlines and decisions.

Panther is designed to keep all of these elements connected to the project they belong to, inside a single structured environment.

Panther is not simply where you edit your work. It is where your research lives.

One environment

From the first draft to the final publication.

Panther treats every research project as a structured object whose documents, presentations, people, events and history belong together.

Philosophy

Your projects remain organized by design.

A Panther project exists inside the project structure managed by Panther itself.

Instead of opening arbitrary files scattered across different locations, Panther becomes the central library for the research projects you deliberately choose to manage with it.

Over time, those projects form a structured database of your research activity: what you worked on, with whom, where it was presented, where it was submitted, and how it evolved.

Papers evolve. Panther remembers how.

A project can contain different versions of a paper across its entire lifetime: early drafts, working versions, submissions, revisions and final versions.

The goal is not simply to store files, but to preserve the history and structure of the project.
