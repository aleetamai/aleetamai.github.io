Panther

A workspace for creating, organizing and managing your reserach projects in one place.

Currently in development

The project

One place for the projects that matter.

Panther is a macOS application designed around a simple idea: research projects should not live as disconnected files and folders scattered across your computer.

Instead, Panther provides a structured environment where projects can be created, organized, opened and managed from a single place.

Panther is not just another editor. It is the place where your projects live.

What Panther does

A unified project environment.

Panther brings together tools for writing, presenting and managing projects while keeping the underlying project structure organized and consistent.

01

Project Manager

Create, organize, rename, archive and manage Panther projects from a central library.

02

Documents

Work with structured text and document projects without losing track of their files and resources.

03

Presentations

Build and manage presentation projects within the same environment used for the rest of your work.

Philosophy

Your projects remain organized by design.

A Panther project exists inside the storage structure managed by Panther itself.

This means that the application is not simply opening arbitrary files from different places. Panther becomes the central library for the projects you deliberately choose to manage with it.

Files created outside Panther can remain outside Panther. But the projects you care about can have a clear, predictable and organized home.

Panther is currently in development.

Panther is being actively designed and tested for macOS. A public download will become available when the application reaches a sufficiently stable release.
